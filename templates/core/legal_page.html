{% if doc_type == 'privacy' %}

01. Data Minimization

MediConnect collects only the personal and clinical fields required to schedule consultations, verify payments, issue prescriptions, and audit hospital ward access: full name, contact phone, email, optional blood group/allergies, consultation notes, and payment transaction references.

02. Row-Level Medical Confidentiality

SOAP medical records, vitals, lab orders, and prescriptions are strictly restricted to the patient, the treating physician who authored the chart, and authorized clinical administrators. General staff and other patients cannot read your medical records.

03. Zero Behavioral Ad Tracking

We do not sell health records or share behavioral telemetry with advertising networks.

{% elif doc_type == 'terms' %}

01. Non-Emergency Telemedicine Scope

MediConnect is designed for scheduled outpatient consultations and follow-up reviews. It is not an emergency dispatch service. Patients experiencing chest pain, severe shortness of breath, stroke symptoms, or acute trauma must attend the nearest emergency department immediately.

02. Clinician Licensing & Key Control

Physician accounts require a single-use MEDI-XXXX access key issued by Hospital Administration and a valid BMDC registration number.

03. Accurate Payment References

Submitting fabricated or duplicate mobile-banking transaction IDs results in payment rejection and account review.

{% elif doc_type == 'refund' %}

01. 72-Hour Advance Booking Window

All telemedicine slots are booked at least 72 hours (3 days) ahead of consultation time so manual MFS and bank transfers can be verified accurately.

02. 24-Hour Cancellation Threshold

Patients may cancel a confirmed appointment up to 24 hours prior to the scheduled consultation start time for refund eligibility. Cancellations requested within 24 hours of consultation start or missed consultations (No-Show) are non-refundable because clinician time has been committed.

03. Rejected or Unmatched Transfers

If a submitted transaction reference cannot be matched in our merchant ledger, Finance marks the entry Rejected with an explanation so you can submit the corrected reference.

{% elif doc_type == 'cookies' %}

01. Strictly Necessary Cookies Only

MediConnect sets only two essential browser cookies:

  • sessionid — keeps authenticated patients, clinicians, and administrators signed in (HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax).
  • csrftoken — protects forms and barcode scan POST endpoints against Cross-Site Request Forgery.

02. Local Storage Preference

A single local key (mc_cookie_ack_v1) remembers that you dismissed the cookie disclosure banner.

{% else %}

01. Visual & Contrast Standards

MediConnect uses dark slate typography (#282D31) on a crisp white canvas (#FFFFFF) and brushed silver chrome (#D9DADC), paired with high-contrast warm yellow primary buttons (#FFC93C + #282D31 ink, exceeding 11:1 contrast).

02. Keyboard, Labels & Print Readiness

All forms include explicit <label> bindings, visible keyboard focus outlines, skip-to-content navigation, and clean A4 print stylesheets for prescriptions and receipts.
